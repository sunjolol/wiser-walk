## Changes

1. **"because more does not quiet wanting" replaced with Ecclesiastes 5:10.** The draft gave
   this as Paul's reason; Paul gives none. The reason now comes from a verse that says it.
2. **"owned by neither" rewritten** to "It is a sentence about a bad month and a good one."
   The original phrase had no clear subject.
3. **"Some teachers in Timothy's church" became "Paul warns Timothy about teachers who ..."**
   The letter does not say whose church they belong to; 6:3 says only "if anyone teaches".
   The quotation now carries the verb from the verse.
4. **Cut "but it is a floor. Nobody is told to be content without food."** It contradicts the
   article's own opening text: Paul says he learned the secret of "being hungry"
   (Philippians 4:12). An inference Scripture does not make.
5. **Luke 12:15 apostrophe** changed to the curly one in the BSB file, so the quotation is
   character for character (the sibling article does the same with Romans 12:19).
6. **"a long retirement" became "years of ease".** Anachronism; the text says "many years.
   Take it easy."
7. **"Every sentence the farmer speaks is addressed to himself" became "In the story the
   farmer talks only to himself."** Same observation (verses 17-19), shorter, and no claim
   about "every sentence".
8. **"forget God" became "deny God"** to match Proverbs 30:9 ("deny You").
9. **"The tenth commandment" became "The commandment against coveting".** Fairness: Catholics
   and Lutherans number Exodus 20:17 as the ninth and tenth, Orthodox and Reformed as the
   tenth. The sentence now lists what the verse lists instead of numbering it.
10. **Cut "A named want is smaller than a vague one"; replaced with "Say which one, in prayer,
    as plainly as the commandment does."** An unsupported psychological maxim.
11. **Rich young man paragraph.** "Catholic and Orthodox teaching hears a counsel ... as monks
    and religious do by vow" became "Catholic and Orthodox Christians have long heard a call
    that some take up literally, as monks and nuns do by vow." "Counsel" as a category
    distinct from precept is Catholic vocabulary that Orthodox writers do not uniformly
    accept, and "religious" as a noun is Catholic jargon. The Protestant sentence stands
    ("generally" is accurate; it is Calvin's reading among others).
12. **"Giving is the most direct way to find out" became "Giving shows".** Unverifiable
    superlative.
13. **"discontent is where both start" became "discontent feeds both".** The stronger causal
    claim is not something the article established.
14. **Cut "We assume a good year takes care of itself" and "God has made"** (after "a
    promise") for length; "Paul's word for the rich" became "Paul's instruction for the rich"
    (the verse is an instruction to Timothy, and the quotation is five words, not a word).
15. **`minutes: 7` became `6`**, matching the published articles of the same length
    (679 and 681 words at 6).
16. Re-wrapped lines; no other wording touched.

## Not changed, on purpose

- No names, dates or non-biblical quotations appear in the article, so there was nothing

- The closing mentions the quiz and the gratitude article without links, as the published
