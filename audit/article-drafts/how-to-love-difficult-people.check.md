## Changes made (7)

1. **"You are nowhere told to enjoy the person's company, and nobody can be ordered to."**
   became "Neither passage tells you to enjoy the person's company." A claim about the whole
   of Scripture is not safe: Romans 12:10 and 1 Peter 1:22 command brotherly affection and
   love "from the heart". The claim is now limited to the two passages under discussion.
   The "nobody can be ordered to" clause also repeated a line already used in the gratitude
   article.
2. **"In the only relationship that finally matters, you were the difficult one."** became
   "In that relationship, you were the difficult one." The original belittled human
   relationships, which the rest of the article treats as mattering, and no verse says it.
3. **"...which may be part of why the command is there."** cut. It was a hedged guess at
   Jesus' motive with no text behind it. "pure contempt" also trimmed to "contempt".
4. **"and we audit it regularly"** cut. Filler; the sentence lands without it.
5. **"not a failure of love"** cut from the Matthew 18 sentence, now "the procedure Jesus
   himself describes." The cut phrase was therapy-register reassurance; the point is made by
   the citation.
6. **Reflowed** the broken line wrapping in the "Do one ordinary good thing" paragraph. No
   wording changed.
7. **Closing line.** "The easy people, as Luke 6:32 points out, prove nothing." became
   "Loving the easy people, as Luke 6:32 points out, is no credit to anyone." The verse says
   "what credit is that to you?", not that such love proves nothing, and the old wording
   read as dismissive of the people themselves.
