## Changes and reasons

1. Psalm 13 claim corrected (see table). Factual error about the text.
2. "hears the identical sentence" changed to "hears the same plea". Matthew 18:26 and 18:29 are not identical sentences.
3. Parable paraphrase: "a sum he can never repay" changed to "ten thousand talents, more than he can pay"; "He is forgiven the lot" changed to "His master forgives the debt". Closer to 18:24-27 and plainer.
4. "The parable turns on a man failing to recognise his own words" changed to "He does not recognise his own words". The parable's own stated point is 18:35 (forgiveness); claiming what it "turns on" was overreach.
5. "has been patient with him for a long time" cut to "has been patient with him". Duration is not in 1 Timothy 1.
6. "The farmer in James 5:7 is not idle; he has ploughed and sown" rewritten. James does not say that; the point is now made as a picture without attributing detail to the verse, and the eschatological context (the Lord's coming, 5:7-8) is stated.
7. James 5:8 quotation widened to include "be patient".
8. "impatience is usually where wrath begins" cut. An unsupported generalisation; replaced with an observation the cited texts do support (James 1:19, Proverbs 16:32, Ecclesiastes 7:8-9 set patience against anger). "a different root" changed to "a second root".
9. "declares his own name" changed to "proclaims" to match Exodus 34:5.
10. "something you do on purpose each morning" cut to "on purpose".
11. "tend to travel together" changed to "travel together" (hedge).
12. Opening two sentences joined with a colon; "In practice the contest is very short" folded into the previous sentence; "which is to say it is" trimmed. Length: draft was 804 words, over the 800 limit.
13. Ragged line wrapping in three paragraphs rewrapped to the house width.
