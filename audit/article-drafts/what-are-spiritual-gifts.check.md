## Changes and why

1. Romans 12:6 and 1 Corinthians 12:4 quotations: ended with a comma inside the quote marks where BSB has a full stop. Recast so the quote ends as the source does.
2. 1 Peter 4:10: "Each" lower-cased to match the source (it is mid-sentence in BSB) and the inserted comma removed.
3. "sorts everything into two kinds" became "names two kinds". Peter gives two examples; he does not claim to classify all gifts.
4. "the ordinary gifts are not put in a lower class" cut. 1 Corinthians 12:28 ranks ("first ... second ... third") and 12:31 speaks of "greater gifts", so the claim is contestable. Replaced with the observable fact: the lists do not separate the spectacular from the everyday.
5. "Here the passages agree entirely" became "Here the passages agree". Only one passage is quoted at that point; "entirely" was more than the evidence shown.
6. "Nor do you choose" now followed by 12:31, "eagerly desire the greater gifts." Without it a Pentecostal reader would fairly say the very next verse was suppressed.
7. "It is no accident that" cut (filler, and a claim about Paul's intent).
8. 1 Corinthians 13: reference widened to 13:8–10 and wording matched to BSB ("cease", "restrained").
9. Hebrews 2:3–4: "the message" became "salvation", which is the subject in the text.
10. 2 Corinthians 12:12: partial quote with altered capital replaced by the verse verbatim.
11. Ephesians 2:20: "the church is" became "God's household", the subject in 2:19; reference moved after the quote so no foreign full stop sits inside it.
12. New paragraph on Catholic and Orthodox Christians. The draft framed the question as a two-sided Protestant debate and left both out. The paragraph claims only what is certain: neither has taught that miracles or healing ended with the apostles, both honour wonder-working saints, and the Catechism of the Catholic Church (paragraphs 801, 2003) counts miracles and tongues among charisms and gives their discernment to the Church's pastors. Paraphrased, not quoted, because the Catechism text is not in the repo to verify character for character. No Orthodox document is cited because none could be verified here. "From every century" was softened to "from many centuries".
13. "Both sides agree" became "All agree", since there are now more than two parties.
14. "the traditions part company" became "Christians part company" (avoids implying whole traditions are uniform; many Baptists and Reformed are continuationist, which the text already signals with "Many").
15. "Holders of this view do not say ..." tightened into one sentence; meaning unchanged.
16. "Scripture gives lists and no test." cut: it repeated the heading directly above it.
17. "Inventories may be useful, but they are a modern tool and should be held as one." cut: "modern" is an undated historical claim with no source, and the sentence hedged.
18. "The grammar of Romans 12 is blunt" became "Romans is blunt" with the verse reference; nothing about grammar was being shown.
19. References added for Romans 12:7 and 1 Peter 4:11, which were quoted without one.
20. "So the practical course" became "The practical course"; commas added around "and will be honest" for reading.
21. Closing: cut "and the Compass will show you where you stand on it" (sales line; the front matter already cross-links the quiz).
22. Opening: "one statement of what the gifts are for, and repeated instruction to use them" tightened to "one stated purpose, and repeated instruction to use the gifts".
23. Second-list lead-in shortened ("A second list in the chapter mixes people and abilities").
24. "Peter is the briefest." cut (filler; also arguable).
25. `minutes: 7` became `minutes: 6`, in line with the published articles of the same length.
