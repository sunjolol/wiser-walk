## Changes

1. `quizzes: []` changed to `quizzes: [seven-deadly-sins]`. The closing section points to
   that quiz, and the published gratitude article cross-links it the same way.
2. "A bridled horse is still a horse. It has not been cured of anything; it is being held,
   daily." became "A bridle does not change the horse; it holds it, daily." The "not cured
   of anything" wording took a side on sanctification that Wesleyan-holiness and many
   Pentecostal readers would reject; the image survives without it.
3. Matthew 5:37: restored the full stop inside the quotation so it is character for
   character.
4. "James repeats it" became "James gives the same instruction"; James 5:12 is not a
   verbatim repeat.
5. "So the psalmist asks for help rather than resolving to try harder" became "So the
   psalmist asks for help". Psalm 39:1 has a psalmist resolving exactly that, so the
   contrast overstated.
6. Oaths paragraph: added the Catholic standard beside the Anglican and Reformed ones, so
   the paragraph no longer reads as a Protestant-only dispute. "Allowing an oath a
   magistrate lawfully requires" became "allowing an oath for a grave reason, as in court",
   which is true of all three documents. "Both agree" became "Both readings agree".
7. Matthew 12:36 sentence split in two and given a bridge ("That is no reason to shrug"),
   because it followed "You will fail at this" with no connection.
8. "James ends his argument the same way" became "in the same place"; James uses springs
   and trees, not the heart-overflow saying itself.
9. Cuts for length and filler: "The point is proportion.", "Two things follow.", "The two
   fit together.", and ", the people who talk for a living" (a gloss James does not make).
   805 words down to 793.
