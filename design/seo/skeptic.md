# THE THREE THAT MATTER MOST
- FIX THE HEADINGS, TITLES AND DESCRIPTIONS ON THE 79 PAGES seo.ts DOES NOT COVER. This is the only finding three auditors reached independently (keywords-and-intent#2, seo-plumbing#1, content-and-linking#8) and the only one I could verify at every step. seo.ts holds 18 path keys and not one is a tradition, figure, gift or bible-figure axis page. So /axis/theology-compass/table/ ships <h1>Table</h1> under a title about real presence versus memorial; /axis/spiritual-gifts/prophecy/ ships <h1>prophecy</h1> in lower case; the National Baptist title is 118 characters; all six /axis/bible-figure/ descriptions are exactly 155 characters and all six end mid-word ('Others wa', 'and tu'). The correct heading string is already computed at axis/[quiz]/[axis].astro:108 and discarded. This is the site's entire long-tail surface, the fixes are one file each, they invent nothing, and they touch no audited wording. Do this before anything else, and do it before requesting indexing, or the quota is spent on the old version. One caution the reports are right about: Hero sizes its ghost word to the heading length, so check 390px before shipping.
- IMPORT THE SITE INTO BING WEBMASTER TOOLS (owner, about 10 minutes). Every one of the seven auditors named this and I verified the mechanism is still live in 2026: Bing offers 'Import from Google Search Console', imported properties are auto-verified with no DNS record or file, and the sitemap comes across. It unlocks Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo and Ecosia in one step, gives a second independent read on what is actually indexed (which is the number that matters right now and which Google reports slowly on a three-week-old domain), and is the precondition for IndexNow being worth anything. Two things nobody mentioned: Bing re-syncs ownership against the Google account periodically, so do not revoke that access, and traffic data takes up to 48 hours to appear. Add Brave's submit-url form (verified live, no account, two minutes) since Brave runs its own index the import does not reach.
- GET THE FIRST HANDFUL OF REAL INBOUND LINKS, AND TREAT IT AS THE HARD PART. A brand search returns the GitHub repo, theocompass.com and a TheologyWeb thread, and nothing from wiserwalk.com. Everything else in this pack is necessary and not sufficient. But do not accept 'post in three or four communities' as executable: the auditor who recommended it could not read the one forum's rules it cited (HTTP 403) and named no venues, and most Christian subreddits remove a new account's first link post on sight. Realistic version: read each venue's rules on the day, post from an account with some history, lead with the game or a specific quiz result rather than the domain, budget four venues to get two that stick, and accept removals without arguing. The cheap reciprocal signals (site link on Ko-fi, README and topics on the repo, sameAs on the Organization node) are worth the twenty minutes but are nofollow or profile fields and will not substitute for this. If this step is skipped entirely, the on-page work still helps on genuinely uncontested long tail (the 19 gift pages, the 25 figure pages) but the denominational head terms stay out of reach.

# CONTRADICTIONS
- THE TRADITION-PAGE DUPLICATION NUMBER. crawl-and-index#1 says 48.2% mean / 68.5% max on 5-word shingles; content-and-linking#2 says '68% identical to each other' citing 68.1% on 6-word shingles for Orthodox vs Calvary Chapel; keywords-and-intent#6 says 36.4% on 8-grams for Orthodox vs Southern Baptist. RULING: crawl-and-index is right. My own run gives Orthodox vs Catholic 68.3% Jaccard at n=5 (their 68.5%), with typical pairs at 43-44%. content-and-linking took the worst-case pair and generalised it to all 18; keywords-and-intent measured a genuinely distant pair on a stricter n and read it as reassurance. The problem is real and large, but the honest number is 'about half, rising to two thirds between neighbouring traditions', not 'two thirds across the board'.
- WHAT TO DO ABOUT THE TRADITION PAGES. crawl-and-index#1 says shrink the boilerplate and build data-driven descriptions. keywords-and-intent#6 says print the audit's confessional citations. content-and-linking#2 says write sourced prose per tradition or else NOINDEX ALL 18. RULING: reject the noindex outright. Eighteen pages backed by a real audited dataset, each about a distinct entity, none functioning as a funnel, are not what Google's scaled-content-abuse policy describes, and noindexing them would delete the entire search surface of the flagship quiz to avoid a risk that has not materialised. The other two are complementary, not competing: crawl-and-index's boilerplate reduction is what actually moves the duplication number, keywords-and-intent's citations add honest depth but only 60-120 words. Do both.
- WHERE THE GAME PROSE GOES. crawl-and-index#5 and content-and-linking#3 both say add 250-500 words below the game on the /play/ pages. seo-plumbing#10 says do NOT touch the play pages and put the words on /games/ instead, because the game document is generated from demos/sounds-like-scripture/game.src.html and hand-editing the committed copy is forbidden. RULING: seo-plumbing is right about the constraint but wrong about the conclusion. The .astro wrapper at site/src/pages/play/sounds-like-scripture.astro is hand-written and editable; only site/src/games/*.html is generated. So prose can go on the play page without touching generated files. Do the easy thing first, though: /games/ is a normal page at 175 words that can carry the prose today with no risk, and it is the page that should rank for 'free Bible games' anyway. Put it there first, and add a short block to the play pages second.
- WHO THE ARTICLE AUTHOR IS. structured-data#2 proposes author as an Organization ('Google states plainly: Use the Person type for people, and the Organization type for organizations... keeps the owner faceless as he wants'). content-and-linking#1 and beyond-google#5 both propose a Person with his real name. RULING: structured-data is right and the other two did not weigh the owner's stated goal. Google's own helpful-content page says E-E-A-T is not a ranking factor and only 'strongly encourages' bylines 'where readers might expect it'. An Organization author is valid markup, satisfies the recommended property, and costs no identity. Take the contact address from content-and-linking#1 and leave the name alone unless he volunteers it.
- IS THE 404 GAP COSTING RESULT LINKS? crawl-and-index#3 and seo-plumbing#8 both claim a bad or expired result link hits Vercel's bare NOT_FOUND string. RULING: both WRONG, and they are wrong the same way, which is how it survived two independent reviews. /r/theology-compass/ZZZZZZ/ serves a designed 22.9KB page titled 'Result not found — Wiser Walk' with the heading 'That link is not a valid result' at a correct 404. Someone already built exactly the recovery page these findings say is missing. A site-wide 404.astro is still worth adding for mistyped URLs, but it is a tidy-up, not the shared-link rescue both reports sold it as.
- IS THE Quiz numberOfQuestions ERROR WORTH FIXING? structured-data#7 calls it the site's only validation error and warns of a sitewide manual action for marking up invisible content. seo-plumbing#15 calls the whole Quiz block harmless and says leave it. RULING: split. structured-data is right on the fact (schema.org/numberOfQuestions returns 404; the property does not exist) so delete the line, it takes a minute. seo-plumbing is right on the consequence: an invalid predicate on one page does not produce a sitewide manual action, and that threat should not be used to rank the work.
- SITEMAP lastmod SCOPE. crawl-and-index#2 wants lastmod across all groups, using mtime or changelog dates for generated pages. seo-plumbing#6 wants it only where a genuinely verifiable date exists (the 11 articles) and explicitly omitted elsewhere. RULING: seo-plumbing. Google uses lastmod only when it is 'consistently and verifiably accurate', and on a site that redeploys on every push, mtime-derived dates for generated reference pages are exactly the inaccuracy that trains Google to discount the field for the whole domain.
- IS DISCOVER BLOCKED? structured-data#3 treats the three sub-1200px article images as blocking the large Discover image. seo-plumbing#11 says Discover is not blocked at all because og:image already carries it. RULING: seo-plumbing is right that nothing is blocked and no markup is required (Google: 'No special tags or structured data are required'), and structured-data is right that 1200px is a stated requirement. Both are arguing over eligibility for a surface this domain will not reach for a long time. Re-export the 511x768 image because it is poor for readers, and drop Discover from the reasoning.
