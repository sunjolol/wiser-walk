# seo-plumbing

The technical floor is genuinely good and better than most new sites: 97 indexable pages, a clean sitemap, correct canonicals on every page including the non-slash variant, robots.txt that deliberately does not block /r/ so link equity flows out of result pages, www 308ing to the apex, BreadcrumbList everywhere, WebSite + Organization on the home page, max-image-preview:large, 42 real og:images, no broken internal links and no orphans. The weakness is not plumbing, it is words. On a three-week-old domain with no backlinks the only winnable queries are long-tail ones, and the long-tail layer is 74 of the 97 pages (18 tradition, 31 axis, 25 figure) whose visible headings name the site's own internal structure rather than the subject: /axis/theology-compass/table/ is about real presence versus the memorial view and its H1 is the single word "Table"; /axis/spiritual-gifts/prophecy/ has the H1 "prophecy" in lower case; not one heading on either page contains the phrase a searcher would type. On top of that the 18 tradition pages carry a templated 75 to 116 character title and a 170 to 211 character description, both truncated in every result, and six bible-figure axis pages ship descriptions cut off mid-word ("Others keep a thought and tu") because the code does a raw slice(0, 155). Six of the 97 pages were rewritten by hand in seo.ts on 21 September and are excellent; the other 91 run on templates that were written for readers already on the site. Separately, the site lives in exactly one index: Bing Webmaster Tools has never been set up, which costs Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, Ecosia and the index that ChatGPT Search and Copilot answer from, and it is a five-minute import from the Search Console account he already verified. Smaller but real: no sitemap lastmod at all, no RSS feed, no 404 page (Vercel serves a plain-text "NOT_FOUND"), the articles hub orders itself differently on Windows and on Vercel's Linux builder because the tie-break that index.astro already has is missing from articles/index.astro, and nothing in the build would catch any of this coming back. The single most important thing is to make the 74 reference pages say what they are about in their own headings and titles, because that is the entire organic strategy for a domain with no authority; the single fastest thing is Bing Webmaster Tools.

## 1. [high impact / medium effort / VISIBLE] The whole reference layer names itself in the site's private vocabulary, not the subject: 74 indexable pages with unsearchable H1s

**Do:** Give every reference page a heading that contains the phrase someone would type, and stop relying on seo.ts to paper over it. In axis/[quiz]/[axis].astro, add a `heading` alongside the existing `title` (line 108) and pass it to Hero instead of group.name: for a bipolar axis `${group.name}: ${group.left} or ${group.right}` ("Table: Real presence or Memorial"), for a gift `The spiritual gift of ${group.name}` ("The spiritual gift of prophecy"), with the two odd ones handled by the existing `group.name.startsWith('the gift of')` branch so "the word of knowledge" becomes "The word of knowledge, as a spiritual gift". ## 6. [medium impact / medium effort] The sitemap carries no lastmod on any of its 97 URLs, so Google has nothing to recrawl on
**Evidence:** Live: curl https://wiserwalk.com/sitemap-0.xml gives 97 <loc> entries and 0 <lastmod> entries. Google's own documentation says "Google ignores <priority> and <changefreq> values" and that it "uses the <lastmod> value if it's consistently and verifiably (for example by comparing to the last modification of the page) accurate" (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap). The @astrojs/sitemap call at astro.config.mjs:39-58 uses only `filter`; the `serialize` hook is not used.

**Do:** Add a serialize hook to astro.config.mjs that stamps a real date where one exists and leaves it off where it does not. The articles already carry a true date in their front matter (published, and an `updated` field should be added to the collection schema at site/src/content.config.ts). Build a map at config time from the article files and set `item.lastmod` for /articles/<slug>/ and for /articles/ (the newest article's date); leave every other URL without one rather than inventing it. Google's guidance is explicit that lastmod has to be verifiable, and stamping all 97 pages with the build date would train it to ignore the field for the whole domain. Do not add changefreq or priority: Google states outright that it ignores both.

**Where:** site/astro.config.mjs:38-59; site/src/content.config.ts (add `updated: z.coerce.date().optional()`)

## 7. [medium impact / small effort / VISIBLE] The articles hub orders itself differently on Windows and on Vercel's Linux builder
**Evidence:** Seven of the eleven articles share published: 2026-09-20 (how-to-be-content, how-to-become-more-humble, how-to-become-more-patient, how-to-forgive-someone-who-hurt-you, how-to-love-difficult-people, how-to-tame-your-tongue, what-are-spiritual-gifts). **Do:** Append the same tie-break to both, so the three call sites agree: `.sort((a, b) => b.data.published.valueOf() - a.data.published.valueOf() || a.data.title.localeCompare(b.data.title))` at articles/index.astro:13 and articles/[slug].astro:66. Without it, the hub's internal link order and the "More to read" block shuffle on every environment change, which is a small but free source of churn in an internal link graph that is the only authority the site currently has.

**Where:** site/src/pages/articles/index.astro:13; site/src/pages/articles/[slug].astro:66 (correct form already at site/src/pages/index.astro:55)

**Do:** Add `author: z.string().default('Wiser Walk')` and `updated: z.coerce.date().optional()` to the articles collection in site/src/content.config.ts, then extend the jsonLd object at articles/[slug].astro:71-79 with `image: [new URL(ogImageFor(`/articles/${article.id}/`), Astro.site).href]`, `author: { '@type': 'Organization', name: 'Wiser Walk', url: 'https://wiserwalk.com/' }`, `dateModified: (article.data.updated ?? article.data.published).toISOString().slice(0, 10)` and `mainEntityOfPage: { '@type': 'WebPage', '@id': url }`.
