## sound
- British spelling throughout: no US forms anywhere in the account pages, the emails, checks.ts, client.ts or either README (grep for color/center/organiz/personaliz/recogniz/canceled found nothing; the README uses "organisation").
- No emoji, no exclamation marks, no flattery, and no "faith" as a noun for the site in any visible string.
- No jargon in visible copy. The words token, session, OTP, verify, authenticate, credentials and "magic link" appear only in comments, ids and variable names; every sentence a person reads is plain ("the emailed link", "the six-digit code", "sign in").
- The "an hour, and only once" claim is true as built: Supabase's email OTP expiry defaults to 3600s and magic links and email OTPs are single-use (research-supabase-auth-flow.md:322-323), and the page makes no network call on load, so a mail scanner cannot spend the token first.
- "Until you choose one, your old password still works" (forgot.astro:58), "Your password has not changed" (magiclink email) and "Your password has not changed until you choose a new one" (recovery email) are all true of resetPasswordForEmail.
- Sign-up and forgotten-password say the same neutral thing whether or not the address exists, and requestReset never uses shouldCreateUser:false, so no page leaks who has an account. One error sentence covers both sign-in fields.
- The marketing line is the brief's exact wording, appears before the field rather than at the foot of the page, and is repeated identically on /me/ — one claim, one form of words, read before anybody types.
- Secondary actions are 44px glass pills at every width (.ac-go, account.css:293), not tiny text links, so the phone rule holds without depending on touch.css.
- The emails keep exactly one link each, differ correctly between HTML and text ("the button below" versus "the link below", and the code inline in the text part), carry no unreplaced template marks and no em dashes, and the preheaders match their subjects.
- /method/ and /about/ no longer promise "no account" or "nothing is stored"; the rewritten "What it keeps" panel lists the address, the codes, the dates and the game values accurately, and "your answers never leave your browser" remains true because scoring is still client-side.
- The analytics hook blanks both the query and the hash for any path under /account/ (Base.astro), /account/* is noindex and out of the sitemap, and the password page adds meta referrer no-referrer, so the one-time token cannot leak by any of the three usual routes.

- The setup page carries one mark, one size and two fills, with the third state in a word ("to do", "needs a look"), which is what the 2026-09-21 rejection asked for; no health sentence contains a key, an address or a count of people.
- Every new short block asks for text-wrap: balance (.ac-rule, .ac-status, .ac-small, .ac-lede, room paragraphs, me-keep paragraphs), and the two-ink headlines end on the accented phrase, which is the site's established pattern rather than an orphan.
