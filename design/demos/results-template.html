The picture first, then the proof, then the depth

Same theme as the locked version. What changed is what the page actually does: a wheel you can read at a glance, bars that fill from the centre so direction and distance are one shape, the axis histories restored, and a share block built around a card rather than a wall of text.

Why the wheel changed

  • Filled sectors, not scattered dots. Six wedges, each split into its two poles; the side you lean fills outward.
  • Area is the message — a big petal is a strong conviction, an empty circle is a centrist. No lines to trace.
  • Six labels, not twelve. Only the pole you actually lean toward gets named.

Why the bars changed

  • They fill from the midpoint out, so length is conviction and side is direction — one read, not two.
  • Eleven pips could not do this. They marked a position but never showed distance from centre.
  • Each axis keeps its own colour, carried through the wheel, the bars and the share card.
