what it is

Today we have 22 early-Christian pages tied to one quiz, 25 Bible people tied to another, and saints named in the personality test with nowhere to go. The hub brings the people together. Each person gets one page that covers their whole life and character, not just what one quiz asks. Every quiz, type page and article then links to it.

why it will rank

  • People search for the person and a question: "is Augustine a saint in the Orthodox Church", "how did Polycarp die", "St Benedict quotes", "John Chrysostom feast day Orthodox".
  • The pages that rank today are thin, list one church's calendar, and rarely say where a quote comes from. Goodreads' most-liked Augustine quote isn't his.
  • We answer each question near the top, give both calendars, source every line, and flag misquotes. Nobody does all four.
  • One page per person, so Google never has two of our pages about the same saint competing.
  • The menu links the hub from every page, which is the strongest internal link a site has.

who comes first

  • Wave 1: the 22 we already have, plus everyone the personality test names who has no page yet (Abba Arsenius, St Monica, St Cuthbert, St Guthlac, St Philip Neri). Isaiah, Jeremiah and Mary of Bethany become Bible pages.
  • Wave 2: the most searched missing saints: St Athanasius, St Ignatius of Antioch, St Polycarp, St Moses the Black (a feature film about him came out this year), St Perpetua, St Ephrem.
  • Later: the big seasonal names (St Nicholas, St Patrick) and the group pages.

how we keep it right

  • Every fact has a source. Every quote is checked word for word against a public-domain text by script, as we did for the quizzes.
  • Both calendars on every page. We check each date against the churches' own calendars. Our notes already disagreed on St Martin's Orthodox date, which is exactly why.
  • Honest labels: "Church Father. Not counted a saint." for Origen, Tertullian and Lactantius.
  • Hard topics are named plainly, in both sides' words, never hidden.
  • No made-up ratings, no tier lists, no prayers to saints. Count knowledge, never devotion.
