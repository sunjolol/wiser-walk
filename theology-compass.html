Your compass

Axis by axis

The marker shows where your answers landed between the two poles. The small tick is the midpoint.

Closest traditions

Approximate positions of typical adherents, not official teaching. Match is 100% minus your distance from that tradition, as a share of the largest distance between any two traditions on this map. These six axes are the questions that split the Western churches at the Reformation: they do not measure church government, the papacy, or the peace-church convictions, so families that differ on those can sit close together here. Broad churches (Anglican, Methodist, mainline) are placed at their center; their wings sit nearer other entries.

A fair summary of both poles, a short dated history, and the passages each side reaches for.
